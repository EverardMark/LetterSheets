Questions, answered

Is my data really encrypted from LetterSheets?

Yes. Sensitive fields are encrypted in your browser before they reach the server, which only ever stores ciphertextData after it has been encrypted — unreadable without the key.. See the security page for details.

Do all plans include every module?

They do — HR, accounting, inventory, fixed assets, sales, procurement, and ticketing are included on every plan. Higher tiers add scale and support.

Can I self-host?

Yes, on the Enterprise plan. Deploy with Docker or on your own server, with configuration and hardening guidance from the team.

What happens after the trial?

Your workspace keeps working — pick a plan when you're ready. Nothing is charged until you choose to subscribe.
