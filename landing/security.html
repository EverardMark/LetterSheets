Your data stays private, even from us.

Most ERPs protect data in transit and trust the server with the rest. LetterSheets encrypts your most sensitive records in your browser and keeps the keys with you.

Zero-knowledge by design

Employee PII, payroll figures, and financial records are encrypted client-side. The server stores ciphertext it cannot read — a breach exposes nothing usable.

Quantum-safe cryptography

Key exchange and signatures use NIST post-quantum standards, so data captured today can't be decrypted by future quantum machines.

You hold the recovery key

Each company gets a downloadable recovery key at setup. Access is unlockable only by you — no silent backdoor, no vendor master key.

Secure from the first login.

Encryption isn't a setting you switch on — it's how the platform works from the moment you create a company.
